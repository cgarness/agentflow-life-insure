## 1. Overview

D3 owns:
- the contracts of KPIs 5–11, 24, 25 and 34 (in `get_report_summary_v3`) and of KPIs 14, 19, 21 and 38 (in `get_report_journey_v3`);
- the call semantics of the four v3 call readers: `get_report_call_volume_v3`, `get_report_disposition_breakdown_v3`, `get_report_campaign_calls_v3` and `get_report_lead_source_calls_v3` (§3.7). D1 writes all nine reader bodies in `…15` with `private.report_v3_metric`, `report_v3_ratio` and `report_v3_rate`, and D3's SQL tests assert the semantics;
- the PKG-A helpers in `…12`, including the attempt classes and `report_v3_identity_numbers`, and the design of the `…16` index (D1 creates that file);
- PKG-C: the `clients.lead_id` guard, the link table, capture with a failure record, a retry RPC and a status read, the `_v2` wrappers, producing-call capture from the Dialer, the FloatingDialer and tracked inbound calls (IN-A, §2.15, with its session-wide `TrackedCallObserver`), and the v3-only window with its release-enable migration `…33`.

Principles:
1. **Calls made counts confirmed attempts (R02, §2.10).** `report_v3_call_facts` classifies every outbound row, after reviewed duplicates are excluded, as `attempt`, `non_attempt` or `unverified`, and sets `is_attempt := direction_class = 'outbound' AND attempt_class = 'attempt'`. Proven non-attempts are excluded and disclosed on demand. Unverified rows are excluded from the value and shown as missing evidence. Raw rows are never changed. Every call surface on the new page uses this one population. Leaderboard, Dashboard, Trusted Dialer and the old Reports tabs keep counting every outbound row.
2. **The contact predicate is exactly the live one.** No Answer is tested first, then > 45 s, then `counts_as_contacted`. The disposition is matched by id, with the org-scoped name fallback only when `disposition_id IS NULL`.
3. **One structural identity.** A prospect is `lead:<root lead>` or `client:<id>`, resolved through same-org links only. There is no `call:` fallback and no phone, email or name matching for identity. `calls.lead_id` is ignored (0 outbound rows set it). Recorded numbers are used only to rule relatedness out (§2.11). They never link, merge, deduplicate or attribute a call (A-38h).
4. **Journeys are org-wide first, then credited** (brief §6). They are computed in two phases (R16) and returned as scoped aggregates under the privacy contract (§2.9: S14a with the BC1 additions, S14b, effects E1–E6). Coverage and `unresolved_activity` count only in-scope credited items. The linked-history design is proposed: ★1 is pending, and no privacy sign-off is claimed.
5. **History gates are metric-specific and member-specific (§2.11, BC1, BC2).** This is a proposed design for isolated development while ★4 is pending. KPI 14 uses only receipt and sale gates. KPIs 19 and 21 keep prospects who never buy: 19 is gated only by possibly related calls that could have qualified as a contact and by the member's own captured phone change, and 21 by any possibly related call and by unverified linked calls. KPI 38 classifies each buyer Yes, No or Unclassified, and uncertainty is never No. Unrelated activity never changes personal or team results. The lookback is the member's whole history before the anchor, not the selected period.
6. **Evidence status and maturity are separate (§2.2).** Among D3's KPIs only 19 carries maturity. Its value is the observed result to date, never blank while members are pending.
7. **1 Call Close needs explicit links.** The PKG-A link stub returns zero rows, so KPI 38 is `unavailable/sale_call_link_not_captured` until the PKG-C window installs the link reader. A missing link is always Unclassified, never "No".
8. **Capture never alters a sale (§2.12).** Errors other than `QUERY_CANCELED` and `ASSERT_FAILURE` are caught, recorded and retried. A cancellation or statement timeout aborts the whole call atomically, and the existing idempotent retry applies.

## 3. Server design

### 3.1 `20261101000012_reports_v3_call_journey_helpers.sql` (PKG-A)

- **Runner stage:** `apply-call-journey`, in one transaction, after `…10`/`…11` and before `…13` (R18). The number is a placeholder; it is renumbered above the production head with the order kept.
- **Privilege class C (§2.8):** no GRANT. Every function gets `REVOKE ALL … FROM PUBLIC, anon, authenticated, service_role`.
- **Guards:**
  - stage header: transaction-local `lock_timeout = '2s'` and `statement_timeout = '30s'` (the runner standard for DDL stages);
  - replay guard: `to_regprocedure('private.report_v3_call_facts(uuid,timestamptz,timestamptz)') IS NULL`;
  - md5(prosrc) of the v2 pins `report_session_facts` 245c7ce4…, `report_session_seconds` 3d33b720… and `report_call_facts` bacbfd06… (full-window class, R17);
  - flags of `report_v3_basis_flags()` and `report_v3_sale_facts(uuid,uuid[],date,date)`;
  - existence of the source tables, including `private.dialer_outbound_admissions`, `private.dialer_conversion_lineage` and `public.contact_history_events`; of `dispositions_org_lower_name_unique`; and of `public.phone_last10(text)` with `idx_calls_org_outbound_contact_phone_last10`.
- **Every function:** owner postgres, SECURITY INVOKER, `SET search_path = pg_catalog, pg_temp`, fully qualified names, created sealed and pinned by md5(prosrc) in `reports_v3_enable.sql` (§2.6). Reached only through D1's SECURITY DEFINER readers.
- **Creation order** (SQL bodies are validated at creation): contact_predicate → local_day_start → client_identity_key → contact_identity → call_facts → identity_receipts → identity_numbers → sale_call_links stub → first_sales → journey_facts.
- **Pins:** the md5(prosrc) of each function feeds D1's generated pin list.

### 3.2 Helpers

**`report_v3_contact_predicate(p_duration_seconds bigint, p_resolved_disposition_name text, p_raw_disposition_name text, p_counts_as_contacted boolean) RETURNS boolean IMMUTABLE`**
```sql
SELECT CASE WHEN lower(coalesce(p_resolved_disposition_name, p_raw_disposition_name, '')) = 'no answer' THEN false
            WHEN coalesce(p_duration_seconds, 0) > 45 THEN true
            WHEN coalesce(p_counts_as_contacted, false) THEN true ELSE false END
```
The order matches 20261006043731:130-136. `report-utils.ts:134-153` is unchanged.

**`report_v3_local_day_start(p_day date, p_time_zone text) RETURNS timestamptz STABLE`** returns `least(p_day::timestamp AT TIME ZONE tz, ((p_day::timestamp - interval '1 hour') AT TIME ZONE tz) + interval '1 hour')`. This is the `report_window` rule (20260929152553:362-405). D3 owns it; D4 calls it.

**`report_v3_client_identity_key(p_org uuid, p_client_id uuid) RETURNS text STABLE`** returns `'lead:'||lead_id` when the same-org client has a lead_id, else `'client:'||p_client_id`, including for a deleted client. D2, D4, D5 and `first_sales` use it.

**`report_v3_contact_identity(p_org uuid, p_contact_ids uuid[], p_campaign_lead_ids uuid[])`** returns `(pointer_kind, pointer_id, identity_key, identity_kind, root_lead_id, client_id, converted_at, resolution)`.
- It uses primary-key and unique lookups only.
- It is the single resolver for `call_facts` and for journey phase 2.
- The first match wins, and every join requires `organization_id = p_org`:

| # | Pointer | Key | resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | recruit type or recruit row | `recruit:`id | `recruit` |
| 2 | client `id = contact_id` | client_identity_key | `client` |
| 3 | lead `id = contact_id` | `lead:`id | `lead` |
| 4 | a client's `lead_id = contact_id` | `lead:`contact_id | `converted_lead_pointer` |
| 5 | `campaign_leads.id = contact_id` (`DialerPage.tsx:2654`) | `lead:`coalesce(cl.lead_id, lineage) | `legacy_membership_as_contact` / `unresolved_membership_without_lead` |
| 6 | contact_id matches nothing (including org 525671d1's 42 cross-tenant calls) | NULL | `unresolved_dangling_contact` |
| 7 | contact_id NULL, campaign membership in the org | `lead:`coalesce(cl.lead_id, lin.original_lead_id) | `campaign_membership` / `campaign_lineage` / `unresolved_membership_without_lead` |
| 8 | otherwise | NULL | `unresolved_no_link` |

`converted_at` = `coalesce(lead_conversion->>'converted_at', created_at)` of the root lead's client. Duplicate lead rows stay separate identities. A call is **unresolved** when its key is NULL (rules 6–8, and rule 5 or 7 without a lead).

**`report_v3_call_facts(p_org uuid, p_start timestamptz, p_end timestamptz)`** covers all agents; callers filter on the credited agent (R5).
- **Rows:** both bounds are required (NULL raises 22023). Same org, `[p_start, p_end)`, reviewed duplicates excluded first. This is exactly the row set of `report_call_facts(p_org, p_start, p_end, NULL)` (test C2).
- **Returns** `(call_id, created_at, direction_class, placing_agent_id, credited_agent_id, duration_seconds, duration_source, duration_conflict, attempt_class, is_attempt, is_contacted, is_answered_inbound, is_journey_contact, identity_key, identity_kind, is_lead_stage, resolution, campaign_id, lead_id, lead_source, disposition_key, disposition_name, disposition_color, disp_counts_contacted, disp_converts, disp_dnc, disp_callback, disp_appointment)`.
- **Credit:** `credited_agent_id` = `agent_id` for outbound calls and `coalesce(agent_id, answered_by_agent_id)` for inbound calls.
- **Attempt classes (§2.10).** Each outbound row is classified by one primary-key lookup in `private.dialer_outbound_admissions (organization_id, call_id)`:

| `attempt_class` | Rule | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `attempt` | `dialer_admission_required = false` (pre-admission rows; brief §4: a missing provider id is not permission to exclude), **or** an admission row with `admitted = true` | Admission row and parent SID; 3,285 of 3,297 admitted rows also carry a provider duration SID |
| `non_attempt` | An admission row with `admitted = false` | The server refused; the webhook returned empty TwiML with no `<Dial>`. Provider duration is 0 on the browser leg |
| `unverified` | `dialer_admission_required = true` and **no** admission row | No provider SID, provider duration, recording or disposition. No PSTN leg could be created, but the provider has not confirmed it |

  Non-outbound rows have `attempt_class` NULL and `is_attempt` false. `is_attempt := direction_class = 'outbound' AND attempt_class = 'attempt'`. Genuine failed attempts (admitted rows with status `failed`, `busy` or `no-answer`) are attempts.
- **Contact flags:**
  - `is_contacted` = outbound AND the predicate (`di` matched by id; `dn` by org lower-case name, only when `disposition_id IS NULL`). It keeps v2 parity (test C2); every v3 call surface counts `is_attempt AND is_contacted`;
  - `is_answered_inbound` = inbound AND (`agent_id` set OR `forwarded_answered`) AND no voicemail;
  - `is_journey_contact` = (`is_attempt` AND `is_contacted`) OR (`is_answered_inbound` AND the predicate).
- **Lead stage:** `is_lead_stage` = lead identity AND (`converted_at IS NULL` OR `created_at < converted_at`).
- **Carried columns:** `campaign_id`, `lead_id`, `lead_source`, the disposition columns and the `disp_*` flags use exactly the pinned `report_call_facts` expressions (test C2). They feed the four call readers (§3.7).

**`report_v3_identity_receipts(p_org uuid, p_root_lead_ids uuid[])`** returns `(root_lead_id, receipt_at, receipt_source)`.
- `receipt_at` is the least() of five sources: `leads.created_at`; `contact_history_events` lead created/imported; `lead_conversion->>'lead_created_at'`; `import_history` (not undone, `imported_lead_ids ? id`); `google_sheets_receipts` imported/imported_flagged.
- Client-only identities get no row.

**`report_v3_identity_numbers(p_org uuid, p_identity_keys text[]) RETURNS TABLE(identity_key text, phone_last10 text, number_source text)`** (new, R10; §2.11).
- **Sources, same org only, current numbers only (RL-19):** the identity's current lead row, its client and its campaign memberships (`campaign_leads.phone`, by `lead_id` and by lineage `original_lead_id`).
- **Normalization:** the existing `public.phone_last10`, the normalizer behind `idx_calls_org_outbound_contact_phone_last10`. A value with fewer than 10 digits gives NULL and is dropped.
- **Use:** only inside `journey_facts` phase 2, only to rule relatedness out for one member and one KPI (proposed design for isolated development; ★4 pending). A number never reaches a payload and never links, merges, deduplicates or attributes a call.
- **Old numbers are not available.** `private.capture_contact_history_event` (20261003192907:96-97) records a phone change only in `changed_fields`; `before_values` and `after_values` hold only `assigned_agent_id`, `status` and `lead_source`. Phase 2 therefore uses the member's own captured change (`phone_changed`, below) for 19, 21 and 38, and changes before 2026-10-03 are an undetectable, disclosed residual.

**`report_v3_sale_call_links(p_org uuid, p_as_of timestamptz) RETURNS TABLE(win_id uuid, call_id uuid, call_direction text, link_source text)`** is the PKG-A stub (`WHERE false`). `basis_flags()->>'sale_call_links'` = `'not_installed'`.

**`report_v3_first_sales(p_org uuid, p_as_of timestamptz)`** is owned by D3 alone; D2 deletes its definition.
- **Reads** `report_v3_sale_facts(p_org, NULL, NULL, NULL)` plus a pass over wins for rows with a NULL `sold_date` or `policy_id`. Only rows with `coalesce(recorded_at, created_at) <= p_as_of` count.
- **Key and order:** keyed by `client_identity_key(p_org, buyer_id)` and ordered by `(sold_date, coalesce(recorded_at, created_at), id)`.
- **Returns** `(identity_key, identity_kind, buyer_id, first_win_id, first_policy_id, first_sold_date, first_seller_id, first_event_time_source, first_set_win_ids uuid[], assessable, reason ∈ {undated_sale, unlinked_legacy_sale})`.

**`report_v3_journey_facts(p_org uuid, p_agents uuid[], p_time_zone text, p_start_at timestamptz, p_end_at timestamptz, p_sold_from date, p_sold_until date, p_as_of timestamptz)`**. The reader passes `now()` and the bounds from `report_v3_sold_window`.
- **Phase 1, candidates (window-bounded):**
  - identities with a journey contact in the window credited to `p_agents` (NULL-credited `forwarded_answered` calls only at agency scope);
  - `first_sales` rows in the sold window whose seller is in `p_agents`.
- **Phase 2, history (identity-keyed, indexed, all history before `p_as_of`):**
  - expand each key to its root lead, its client (`uq_clients_lead_id`) and its memberships (by `lead_id` and by lineage `original_lead_id`, via D5's index);
  - fetch linked calls by contact_id (`contact_history_calls_page`) and by campaign_lead_id (`report_v3_calls_org_campaign_lead`), re-resolve them through `contact_identity` and keep the rows with the same key. Facts use `call_facts`' expressions (test J13);
  - **candidate unresolved calls:** rows that are unresolved under `contact_identity` (no link, or a dangling pointer), by any agent, created before the anchor at any time (not only inside the window), whose pinned v2 `direction_class` is `outbound` or which are answered inbound calls (`is_answered_inbound`, caller number in `contact_phone`). Unanswered inbound calls never count (RL-22). Raw `direction = 'outbound'` rows are read through `idx_calls_org_outbound_contact_phone_last10`; answered inbound rows and other outbound spellings come from an org-bounded read of unlinked rows (`contact_history_calls_page` with `contact_id IS NULL`) plus the dangling pointers;
  - **could have qualified as a contact:** a candidate that is not a confirmed non-contact under the live predicate: it has a qualifying disposition, a recorded duration > 45 s, or no recorded duration, and its resolved disposition is not No Answer (A-§5 order);
  - **possibly related call:** a candidate whose `phone_last10(contact_phone)` equals one of the member's current numbers from `identity_numbers` and that precedes the member's anchor (for KPI 38, the linked sale call). It is exclusion evidence only;
  - **the member's own captured phone change:** a `contact_history_events` row for the member's own lead or client with `phone` in `changed_fields`, before the anchor (for KPI 38, before the linked sale call). It never depends on any other activity in the organization (BC1);
  - **numberless unresolved calls:** candidates with `phone_last10(contact_phone) IS NULL` before the latest member anchor. They are read only for unfiltered agency scope (BC1);
  - **in-scope unresolved activity:** unresolved calls credited to `p_agents` (every row at agency scope) created before the latest member anchor, which is the union of the members' lookbacks.
- **Output:** one classification row per candidate and KPI, plus the counts. No key, number, timestamp or call id reaches a payload.
- **First contact:** the earliest journey contact by any agent (ordered by `created_at, call_id`).
- **Attempts (KPI 21):** prior `is_attempt` calls by any agent, plus 1 if the first contact was outbound; non-attempts never count. An inbound-first contact may have 0.
- **History gates (§2.11, BC1, BC2; proposed design for isolated development, ★4 pending).** A gate makes a member unassessable for one KPI, and only on evidence specific to that member and that KPI. A phone number is exclusion evidence only: it never links identities, attributes a sale or deduplicates, and the absence of a match is not proof that the history is complete ("assessable" means no conflicting recorded evidence). The first failing gate, in the order listed, gives the member's `detail.history.unassessable_by` key for that KPI:

- **Numberless unresolved calls** (0 today) cannot be shown unrelated. At personal and team scope, and with an agent filter, they have no effect (BC1). They never affect KPI 14.
- **Future Sold Dates** count only once the date arrives (R23).
- **Contact outcome (19)**, tested in order:
  1. `prior_buyer`: Sold Date before the anchor. The person is not a member of 19 or 21 (E1);
  2. `unassessable` (KPI 19's gates);
  3. `success`: Δ in [0, 30] days, recorded ≤ `as_of`;
  4. `mature_no_sale`: `as_of >= local_day_start(anchor + 31)`. A prospect who never buys ends here, as a non-success;
  5. `open_no_sale_yet`: the window is still open and there is no success yet. The maturity block's `pending` count is wider: it counts every member whose window is open, including early successes.
- **1 Call Close**, tested in order:
  1. first sale not assessable → **Unclassified** (`history_incomplete`);
  2. no explicit sale-call link, or a link whose call is absent or belongs to another identity → **Unclassified** (`sale_call_link_missing`);
  3. a **confirmed** qualifying contact (a linked journey contact by any agent) before the linked call → **No**;
  4. before the linked call, a possibly related call that could have qualified, or the member's own captured phone change → **Unclassified** (`history_incomplete`): uncertainty, never an automatic No;
  5. the linked call is the member's first qualifying contacted call → **Yes**. A confirmed earlier unanswered or non-contact attempt does not disqualify it;
  6. otherwise (the linked call is not a qualifying contact) → **Unclassified** (`sale_call_link_missing`).

  Inbound links count, including those captured through IN-A. An earlier contact by another agent still decides which contact is first (E1).

### 3.3 KPI contracts

- **Envelope:** §2.2, built only through D1's builders (`report_v3_ratio`, `report_v3_rate`, `report_v3_total`, `report_v3_median`), which take status from `report_v3_evaluate` and validate through `report_v3_metric`; a violation raises P0001 and fails only that panel, closed. Reasons come only from `report_v3_reason_codes()`, and each emitted code is in its §2.3 KPI column. Detail field names are exactly those of §2.4.

- **Status order (§2.2 BC4 truth table, in the RL-40 order):** config code → empty eligible population (zero code) → everything unassessable (first applicable coverage code) → zero denominator among the assessable items (zero code) → partial when any unknown part > 0 or a window-level condition applies → available. The denominator decides first: known 0 with unknown 0 gives the zero code; known 0 with unknown > 0 gives the first applicable coverage code. Then the numerator: known 0 with unknown > 0 gives a coverage code, never a fabricated 0. A complete 0 over a positive denominator is a valid value.

**KPI 24 and 25 quality (exact definitions, PKG-A Batch 5).** Both KPIs are `available` whenever their scope reads (an empty window is `available 0`); quality is disclosed in `detail` on the totals only, never used to upgrade or drop a duration, and the duration writers are untouched.

**Coverage codes of the journey KPIs.** A member that fails one of its KPI's history gates counts under `lead_receipt_unknown` when it has no recorded lead start (`unassessable_by` key `lead_receipt_unknown` or `hand_entered_client`), and under `history_incomplete` otherwise. For KPI 14, `history_incomplete` therefore means a contradicted receipt, a receipt after the first sale, or a first sale that is not assessable (`receipt_contradicted`, `sold_before_receipt`, `imported_already_sold`); it is never triggered by call history, phone changes or numberless calls (BC2). For 19 and 21 it also covers `possibly_related_unlinked_call` and `phone_changed`. KPI 21 counts members with an unverified linked call before the first contact under `attempt_unverified`. KPI 38 counts Unclassified buyers under `history_incomplete` (first sale not assessable, or uncertain order) or `sale_call_link_missing` (no usable link), so it never emits `lead_receipt_unknown`.

**KPI 38 status**, tested in order:
1. `report_v3_basis_flags()->>'sale_call_links' = 'not_installed'` (the PKG-A stub) → `unavailable/sale_call_link_not_captured`.
2. Options (c) or (b′) below agency scope → `unavailable/journey_scope_restricted` (D1 reader gating).
3. No first-time buyer in the window (eligible = 0) → `unavailable/no_first_time_buyers`.
4. Yes + No = 0 → unavailable with the first applicable coverage code in registry order: `history_incomplete`, then `sale_call_link_missing`. Coverage carries `assessable = 0` and every count.
5. Any Unclassified buyer, or the numberless rule at unfiltered agency scope → partial with the first applicable coverage code.
6. Otherwise available.

The page never claims that history is missing when only the link is absent (brief §8): a link-only gap carries only `sale_call_link_missing`.

**KPI 19 maturity (§2.2 rule 7, BC4).** The block is attached whenever the cohort has members (members > 0), whatever the status, so known pending-outcome metadata is never discarded. Under `journey_scope_restricted` no cohort is computed, so there is none. A first-contact prospect who has not bought is a valid member: pending until mature, then a non-success.
- `maturity = {window_days: 30, state, members, pending, successes_to_date, mature_members, mature_successes, fully_mature_on}`.
- **Whole group (§2.2 rule 7).** `members` = every first-contact member, assessable or not, so the block survives an unavailable value; `pending` = members with `as_of < local_day_start(anchor + 31)`; `mature_members` = `members` − `pending`; `state = 'in_progress'` iff `pending > 0`; `fully_mature_on` = max anchor + 31 (agency date). `successes_to_date` and `mature_successes` count only verified successes of assessable members, so when unassessable members exist the mature-only subset is a lower bound, and the details say so.
- `value` = `successes_to_date` ÷ assessable members × 100: numerator `successes_to_date`, denominator the assessable members (`coverage.assessable`), never `maturity.members`. It is the observed result to date over the assessable cohort: pending assessable members stay in the denominator, and no early successes are selected. With unassessable members, `maturity.members` is larger than the denominator.
- `mature_members` and `mature_successes` count only members whose window has elapsed. They appear in details and in the CSV under their own column names, never as a second rate under the metric's name. D6's CSV writes Basis `observed_to_date` and In progress `yes`/`no` for KPI 19 (RL-5).
- A window with no first contacts is `unavailable/no_first_contacts`, whether or not members are pending. First contacts that are all unassessable give the first applicable coverage code instead.

**`detail.history`** (14, 19, 21, 38) = `{assessable, unassessable_by: {lead_receipt_unknown, receipt_contradicted, possibly_related_unlinked_call, phone_changed, sold_before_receipt, imported_already_sold, hand_entered_client}, numberless_unresolved_calls}` (§2.4, RL-25). Each KPI counts only its own gates (§3.2), so KPI 14 never shows `possibly_related_unlinked_call` or `phone_changed`, and 19 and 21 never show `sold_before_receipt` or `imported_already_sold`. Undated or unlinked legacy first sales count under `imported_already_sold`, with `sold_date_proxy`. `numberless_unresolved_calls` is the org-level count for 19, 21 and 38 at unfiltered agency scope only; otherwise, and always for 14, it is null (BC1).

**`detail.unresolved_activity`** (14, 19, 21, 38) = `{outbound_attempts, contacted_calls, answered_inbound}`: in-scope credited unresolved calls within the members' lookback, which is all history before the latest member anchor and not only the selected window. Below agency scope it counts only calls credited to `agent_ids`; it is org-level only when `agent_ids IS NULL`. Out-of-scope agents' unresolved calls never enter it.

### 3.4 Journey privacy (R04, BC1, §2.9; D1-06 owns the pending decision ★1)

- **Definitions.** An identity is *in scope* for a payload when it has at least one event credited to an in-scope agent that enters one of that payload's populations. It is *unrelated* when it has no event credited to an in-scope agent at any time. Cross-organization rows are never read.
- **Status (★1 pending).** This is the proposed option (a) linked-history design, developed in isolation with BC1 and BC2 applied. Production acceptance of the disclosures and the named security review of the exact SQL are pending, and no reviewer has been appointed. No privacy sign-off is claimed.
- **S14a, unrelated identities.** Adding activity on unrelated identities by out-of-scope agents leaves every personal and team payload of the summary, journey and the four call readers byte-identical apart from `as_of`. D3's fixtures cover calls of every attempt class, contacted calls, unresolved calls, sales and links, plus matching activity in another organization.
- **S14a, BC1 additions.** Two more fixtures must leave every personal and team value, status, coverage block and reason code unchanged; only unfiltered agency-scope diagnostics may change:
  - adding or removing an unrelated out-of-scope numberless unresolved call;
  - adding or removing unrelated unresolved activity elsewhere in the organization while an in-scope person has a captured phone change.

  Organization-wide unknown-history diagnostics appear only at authorized, unfiltered agency scope.
- **S14b, linked in-scope identities.** Out-of-scope activity on an in-scope identity may change a D3 KPI or breakdown only through these effects (A-38s, pending):

| Effect | D3 KPIs | What may change |
|---|---|---|
| E1 earlier contact or sale | 14, 19, 21, 38 | The person leaves the in-scope first-contact or first-time-buyer population, or the 1 Call Close result becomes "No" |
| E2 earlier attempts | 21 | Out-of-scope outbound attempts before the first contact count in that person's attempt count |
| E3 purchase from anyone | 19 | A purchase from any seller within 30 days on the same identity counts as a success |
| E5 possibly related unlinked call | 19, 21, 38 coverage (never 14) | Member-specific evidence only. An unresolved call by any agent to a number recorded for the member, before the member's anchor, makes that member unassessable for the KPIs whose gate it touches: 19 only if the call could have qualified as a contact; 21 for any call; 38 only if it could have qualified before the linked sale call. The member's own captured phone change is member-specific evidence of the same kind (19, 21, 38). Numberless calls and other members' activity are **not** E5 effects; they never change personal or team results (BC1). Nothing is attributed to the person |
| E6 lead assignment | Lead-source breakdown, "New leads" | New leads counts leads currently assigned to in-scope agents (the v2 rule, unchanged), so a reassignment by anyone changes it |

- **Tests in both directions** (D1's `reports_v3_security.sql` on D3 fixtures): for each of E1, E2, E3, E5 (a matching call that could have qualified, for 19 and 38; a matching non-qualifying call, for 21 only; the member's own phone change) and E6 (a reassignment of a lead created in the window), a fixture asserts the exact change and that nothing else changes (no new `by_agent` rows, no ids or names, no other KPI or row); removing the activity restores the payload byte-identically; a negative fixture (out-of-scope calls after the anchor, appointments, purchases outside 30 days) leaves the payload unchanged.
- **Never exposed:** row identities, phone numbers, timestamps or other agents' records; out-of-scope agents' names or rows; names or metadata of campaigns not visible to the viewer; an unattributed bucket below agency scope; cross-organization data (test J7).
- **Filtered comparisons.** Differencing adjacent windows or agent filters can isolate one person's linked effect. Proposed option (a) accepts that for E1–E6 within the viewer's own credited population; (b′) narrows it to whole agency months with n ≥ 5; (c) removes E1–E3 and E5 below agency scope (`journey_scope_restricted`), while E4 and E6 stay. Options (c) and (b′) are D1 reader gating; the helpers do not change.
- **Review gate.** A named security reviewer reviews the actual SQL of every reader body and the permission test results (S1–S15, S14a with the BC1 additions, S14b) on the exact commit, before the reader bodies are frozen and before the PKG-A production approval. ## 10. Risks

- Unlinked dials (41.6% of attempts since 2026-10-04) now make matching members unassessable for the KPI whose gate they touch (any match for 21; only calls that could have qualified for 19 and 38) instead of producing a false Yes or an undercount. Residual limits stay disclosed: numbers changed before 2026-10-03, shared household numbers (conservatively unassessable) and contact made outside AgentFlow. The absence of a match never shows that nothing is missing.
- Old phone numbers are not recorded, so a member's own captured phone change makes that member unassessable for 19, 21 and 38 (count at preflight); it never depends on other activity. Changes before 2026-10-03 stay undetectable (disclosed).
- ★4 and ★1 are pending. The number-based gates and the linked-history effects are a proposed design for isolated development; if either decision goes the other way, they must be replaced before the reader bodies are frozen.
- Answered inbound calls and outbound rows not spelled `outbound` are read outside the partial number index, through an org-bounded read of unlinked rows; the journey gate covers it.
- `TrackedCallObserver` runs on every page for the whole session; it only reads public TwilioContext values and writes nothing.
- `unresolved_activity` over the whole lookback is a history count per reader call; the journey gate (p95 ≤ 4 s) covers it.
- Proposed option (a) needs ★1 acceptance and the named security review of the actual SQL; no reviewer is appointed yet.
- Windows that contain unverified rows show Calls made as Partial; the 12-row difference from other surfaces is disclosed in one line.
- Talk-time conflicts show only in How it's calculated.
- A change from #486 breaks the `…31` guard; re-pin.
- A missed `lead_id` writer would get 42501.
- The performance gate may fail.
- Phase-2 logic may drift from `call_facts` (J13 checks).
- If PKG-D lands first, `…32` must be re-pinned and PKG-C rebases the shared modals.
- Harness columns may drift from production (`answered_by_agent_id`, `voicemail_id`, `dialer_admission_required`); fixtures add them explicitly.

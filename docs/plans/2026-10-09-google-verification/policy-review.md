## Privacy Policy

### Who we are

AgentFlow is operated personally by Christopher Garness, also known as Chris Garness. AgentFlow provides customer relationship management and communications software for life insurance agencies at www.fflagent.com. This notice explains how we handle personal information through the service and its optional Google integrations.

Your agency determines how it uses its workspace, manages its members and handles the client information it supplies. Contact your agency about its own insurance, marketing and recordkeeping practices; contact AgentFlow about the service and Google information described here.

### Information we collect and use

We process account and profile information, contact details, agency membership and permissions, and records that you or your agency enter or import. These may include leads, clients, insurance-policy information, notes, tasks, appointments and communication history. Calling and messaging features can also process phone numbers, message content, call details and recordings when recording is enabled.

We use this information to provide the features you request, operate accounts and agency workspaces, support users, troubleshoot problems and protect the service. Technical request and error information may be recorded for these purposes. Browser storage supports sign-in and preferences.

### Gmail information

If you connect Gmail, AgentFlow requests your Google account identity, name, email address and basic profile, permission to read email, and permission to send email on your behalf. It uses that access to send messages you compose in AgentFlow, retrieve email conversations, match messages to CRM contacts and display communication history.

Imported information can include sender and recipient addresses, subjects, message bodies, timestamps and message or conversation identifiers. Synchronization can run in the background while you are signed out. Initial synchronization retrieves a limited set of recent messages; that import window is not a deletion or retention period.

The import can include messages that do not match a CRM contact. Connecting a personal mailbox can therefore bring personal messages into your agency workspace. Use an account that you are authorized to connect and whose information may be handled in this way.

### Google Calendar information

Google Calendar is an optional integration for listing available calendars and synchronizing appointments with a selected calendar. Depending on the enabled settings, AgentFlow reads event details or creates and updates Google Calendar events from AgentFlow appointments. Event information can include titles, descriptions, dates, times, attendees, locations and event identifiers.

The current authorization flow requests calendar access and event access. The Google consent screen shows the permissions being requested. Google or your Google Workspace administrator may limit the permissions you can grant.

### Google data and Limited Use

AgentFlow uses Google information to provide the email, communication-history and calendar features that users choose. AgentFlow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

We do not sell Google information or use it for advertising, advertising audiences, creditworthiness or lending decisions, or training generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. These restrictions also apply to information derived from Google data.

Google information is shared only as needed for the user-facing features you authorize, security, applicable legal obligations, or a business transfer with your prior consent as required by Google's policy. AgentFlow personnel may access specific Google information with your affirmative permission, when necessary for security or legal obligations, or in the other limited circumstances permitted by that policy.

### Agency visibility and service providers

Imported communications become agency workspace records. Other authorized users, including agency leadership where the applicable access rules allow it, may see those records and related activity or notification previews. Connecting a mailbox is not intended as private storage accessible only to the mailbox owner.

Supabase supplies application databases and backend processing; Vercel hosts the application; Google processes Gmail and Calendar requests. Twilio supports calling and text messaging, and Resend supports AgentFlow system email. These providers process information needed for the relevant services. Connecting Gmail does not itself authorize transferring Gmail content into an unrelated service or feature.

We may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to investigate abuse and protect users or the service. Google information remains subject to the Limited Use restrictions above.

### Storage and safeguards

AgentFlow stores imported information and connection information in its hosted application systems. OAuth access and refresh tokens allow the service to use the Google permissions you grant without receiving your Google password. Information is transmitted through HTTPS connections, and the application uses sign-in and access controls to restrict access to agency records.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. This notice does not represent that Google has verified AgentFlow, that an independent security assessment has been completed, or that a particular security certification applies.

### Disconnecting and revoking Google access

You can disconnect Gmail in AgentFlow's Email Setup settings. The disconnect operation marks the connection disconnected and clears its saved access and refresh tokens. Previously imported emails and related CRM history remain unless deleted separately. Disconnecting is not a request to delete your Google mailbox or a guarantee that work already in progress has been cancelled.

To revoke Google's authorization, remove AgentFlow through your Google Account's third-party connections page. Revocation can affect both Gmail and Calendar when they share a Google authorization. Google account controls and AgentFlow's local disconnect control perform different actions.

### Retention and deletion requests

We retain information while it is needed to provide the service, maintain agency records, address security issues or meet applicable obligations. Imported Google history is not automatically deleted when you disconnect. You can request access, correction or deletion by emailing the support address below and identifying your account, agency and the information concerned. Do not include passwords, access tokens or unrelated mailbox content.

We verify the requester's identity and authority before acting. Our service target is to complete covered deletion requests within 30 calendar days after verification, sooner where required, and confirm the result. Requests involving shared agency records, retention obligations or records whose source needs confirmation require review. We will explain any material limitation or delay; submitting a request does not itself erase information.

For departing agents, access removal and disconnection are handled with the agency; requests concerning personal Google information are reviewed separately from shared client records. For workspace closure, our service target is a 30-day export window before deletion unless an applicable retention obligation requires otherwise. An explicit deletion request does not have to wait for that export window.

Backup and other retained copies are handled separately from live records. Our target is for covered deleted information to expire from backups within 30 days after live deletion. Backup expiry and any other retained copies must be checked before we confirm complete removal. We do not promise immediate deletion from every copy when a live record is removed.

Deleting AgentFlow's imported Gmail records does not delete messages from your Google mailbox. A later reconnection can import messages again. Google and your agency may separately retain records under their own practices and obligations.

- Printed/PDF p13: pulmonary hypertension, Ever, Select. This is a separate identity, not a synonym for ordinary/systemic high blood pressure.
- The current web renderer again returned older Select-only pages at the same address while parsed text reported the newer guide. No mixed-version rules were added. The existing pinned, locally verified 30-page PDF is the baseline; source refresh/version reconciliation remains a separate task.

High blood pressure, High BP, HBP, HTN and hypertension normalize to a single term; recognition itself is not eligibility. Pulmonary arterial hypertension/HTN stays separate. High cholesterol, low blood pressure, underactive/overactive thyroid and nonspecific arthritis are recognized identities only; no new carrier outcomes were invented for them. Medication recognition never implies a diagnosis.

Americo's approved 11/25 guide and Mutual of Omaha's approved April 2026 Living Promise source were checked for scope. No explicit final-expense hypertension classification was established from them for this change; their known source/state/tier limitations remain yellow. The hypertension/build combinations in Mutual's Term Life Express/IUL sections do not become Living Promise rules.

## Product assumptions — not source rules
The omission-as-absence policy is Chris's quick-screen design assumption, not a published carrier rule or a completed application. The adapter makes a cloned case only; captured facts and native evaluator defaults stay intact. Unknown text and missing listed-condition qualifiers cannot be cleared by the assumption.

No approval probability or compensation outcome is inferred. The UI still requires actual verified schedules before commission ordering.

Historical BP control is not treated as current control. Numerical BP readings are retained as an unassessed qualifier rather than silently ignored; no clinical measurement thresholds were added. Both cases remain yellow without a mandatory question.

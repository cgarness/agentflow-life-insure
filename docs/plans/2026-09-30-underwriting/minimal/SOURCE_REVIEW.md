## Declared medication context — inference, not a carrier-approved-drug list
The narrow quick-screen adapter recognizes metformin alongside already entered diabetes, literal insulin alongside already entered diabetes, and lisinopril alongside exactly one already entered matching hypertension/heart-failure/heart-attack context. These establish a coherent declared treatment context for preliminary placement only, not a guaranteed acceptable prescription. Carrier review/exclusion rules remain intact in the native results. Off-label/alternative, unknown, discontinued and multi-indication ambiguity are not silently cleared. Medication recognition never inserts a disease. A conflicting explicit No insulin is not overwritten.

# Live execution decision

**Hold scheduled execution, enrollment and provider handoffs.** The release can expose the authoring surface separately. The November 21 inspection found no attributable production caller for the workflow worker and no verified automatic path that supplies and refreshes every fact needed to send to a newly arriving lead. These decisions apply to Supabase project `jncvvsvckxhqgqvkppmj` and the AgentFlow release under review.

The verified production state is an active `workflow-resume-paused` v44 package with `verify_jwt=false` and internal-secret authentication. Its caller, operational owner and recurrence remain unverified. The archived schema includes a commented five-minute example. The legacy source and audit repeat that interval. ## Eligibility of newly arriving leads

A committed lead insert records the organization, typed contact, lead source and event time when intake is enabled. Membership events carry the campaign identity of the committed attachment. The worker reads the current contact again at the step, verifies the publisher's audience authority, and checks the current destination immediately before handoff. Capture and enrollment do not prove a send is eligible.

| Required fact | Available evidence and missing integration |
| --- | --- |
| Agency channel policy and sending hours | The server checks an enabled policy, shared agency quota, recipient-local sending window and relevant reply freshness. No production writer for `private.automation_channel_policies` was identified in the candidate. The policy owner, source, configuration changes and verified feed-health updates need an operational path. |
| Recipient identity, eligibility and timezone | `private.automation_recipient_facts` binds an expiry and verified timezone to the exact organization, typed contact, channel and destination. Email also requires affirmative permission. No automatic producer, refresh job or revocation integration was identified for this table. State-based defaults used elsewhere in the CRM cannot verify the recipient's zone. Tennessee, for example, spans two time zones. |
| SMS consent and sender | The protected SMS path rechecks purpose-specific consent, confirmation where required, suppression, DNC, sender readiness and sticky identity. The consent bridge sends a signed request. It checks the returned organization, profile, E.164 destination, purpose, evidence and 30-second freshness bound. Live package metadata confirmed `sms-consent-events` v26, `sms-consent-worker` v28 and `twilio-sms-webhook` v48. These services cover SMS evidence and phone state. They do not populate the separate verified timezone or agency policy required by the new automation worker. |
| Email sender, suppression and replies | Email requires a current `private.automation_email_senders` record, authorized From address and monitored Reply-To mailbox. No automatic producer for verified sender records was identified. A connected mailbox alone is insufficient. Live `email-sync-incremental` v54 records reply headers and a last-sync timestamp. Its code can report individual insert/cursor errors while updating that timestamp, so additional evidence is required for contiguous inbound coverage. The candidate receipt handler supports attributable bounces, complaints and unsubscribe events. Pre-existing suppression coverage, continuous feed health and repair of gaps require verification. The candidate `automation-provider-receipt` package was absent from the inspected live inventory. |

A PostgreSQL 17 fixture exercised the actual authenticated lead insert followed by admission in a later transaction. A fresh agency had zero verified senders. A connected Google mailbox ID was rejected as a workflow sender. Handoff first reported **Agency sending limits and local sending hours require verification**. Supplying the agency policy exposed the next hold, **Current recipient eligibility or verified timezone is missing**. Synthetic SMS ingress recorded one informational grant, one marketing refusal and one pending confirmation job. It produced no verified recipient facts or reply-health watermark. Across 15 assertions there were zero provider handoffs, receipt rows, SMS dispatches or projected emails. The network-free SMS test also rejected stale and incorrectly scoped consent responses. ## Conditions for a production decision

1. Obtain a control-plane record for the current caller or a documented inventory from the operations owner establishing that a new caller is needed. Record scheduler system, owning team, job ID, enabled state, target project and worker, cadence, timezone, previous and next run, retry policy and on-call contact. Compare the record against independent invocation metadata. Resolve potential relays and duplicate callers before approving any schedule.
2. Assign owners and authoritative producers for each agency policy, per-destination eligibility fact, verified timezone, email sender identity, suppression source and reply feed. Specify provenance, freshness limit, expiry, revocation and recovery after missed updates. Keep verified opt-out and sender readiness on their established paths. Changes of destination or authority must invalidate stale evidence.
3. In an isolated organization, start with an ordinary newly created lead. Exercise the real integrations through initial acquisition, expiry, revocation, changed destination, stale feed and recovery, with synthetic transports only. Check both consent purposes, both channels independently, monitored replies, late receipts, restart and repair. Hand-inserting facts validates the consumer only.
4. Size the single due-work caller against measured event and step volumes, backlog recovery and acceptable wait lateness. The candidate returns claimed due lag, stage failures and intake/claim/repair counts. Record alerts, target latency and responsible responders. Preserve legacy wait handling when changing the owner or cadence.
5. Request separate approval for an exact production manifest after the operational prerequisites pass. Identify database changes, complete Edge packages and auth settings, sender/receipt integrations, scheduler record, Vercel release and independent visibility/enrollment/dispatch gates. Recheck installation for zero new definitions, enrollments and sends. Installation never activates an agency workflow.
